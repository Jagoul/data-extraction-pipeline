# Text-message coaching and type 2 diabetes: findings from primary care

Chidi Mensah

Northern Journal of Public Health · 2020 · https://doi.org/10.5555/jace.2020.7213

## Introduction
Interest in this area has grown [1, 2], with mixed findings across settings [3].

## Methods
We conducted a case-control study following established guidance [4]. A total of 118 participants were included in the analysis. Our primary outcome was change in HbA1c at 12 months.

## Results
Effects favoured the intervention and were robust to sensitivity analyses [5-11].

# Remote weight monitoring for heart failure: a discrete choice experiment in community settings

Aisha Achterberg, Zanele Almeida, Jonas Albrecht



## Introduction
Interest in this area has grown [1, 2], with mixed findings across settings [3].

## Methods
We conducted a discrete choice experiment following established guidance [4]. The analysis included 60 participants. Our primary outcome was 30-day readmission.

## Results
Effects favoured the intervention and were robust to sensitivity analyses [5-14].

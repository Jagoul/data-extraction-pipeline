# Group physiotherapy for chronic low back pain: a case-control study in rural clinics

Maria Ibsen

Journal of Applied Clinical Evidence · 2015

## Introduction
Interest in this area has grown [1, 2], with mixed findings across settings [3].

## Methods
We conducted a case-control study following established guidance [4]. The analysis included 118 participants. Our primary outcome was disability score at 3 months.

## Results
Effects favoured the intervention and were robust to sensitivity analyses [5-13].

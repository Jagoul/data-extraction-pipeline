# Clinician audit and feedback and antibiotic prescribing: findings from three urban hospitals

Ravi Albrecht

Published in Annals of Community Medicine, 2013.
doi:10.5555/ghm.2013.4565

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Marchetti et al., 2012; Mbeki et al., 1998), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a case-control study. The analysis included 312 participants. The primary outcome was antibiotic prescription rate.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Novak et al., 2011; Almeida et al., 2008).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

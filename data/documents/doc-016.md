# Remote weight monitoring and heart failure: findings from rural clinics

Priya Mensah, Priya Haddad, Lucas Bianchi, Chidi Brennan, Hana Obi

Clinical Outcomes Quarterly · 2019 · https://doi.org/10.5555/coq.2019.3340

## Introduction
Interest in this area has grown [1, 2], with mixed findings across settings [3].

## Methods
We conducted a randomised controlled trial following established guidance [4]. A total of 4,512 participants were included in the analysis. Our primary outcome was 30-day readmission.

## Results
Effects favoured the intervention and were robust to sensitivity analyses [5-11].

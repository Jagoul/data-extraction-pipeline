# Light-exposure scheduling and sleep problems in shift workers: findings from community settings

Kwame Whitfield, Sofia Szabo, Siobhan Mbeki, Maria Varga, Noor Eriksen, Leila Yamada

Published in Evidence and Practice, 2014.

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Delacroix, 2007; Moreau, 1999; Varga, 2003), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a qualitative interview study. The analysis included 24 participants.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Osei and Obi, 2005; World Health Organization and Kaur, n.d.; Ferreira and Holloway, 2003).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

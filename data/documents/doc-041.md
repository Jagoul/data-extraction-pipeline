# Telephone befriending for depression in older adults: a prospective cohort study in community settings

Ravi Kaur, Noor Ibsen, Mateo Ncube, Maria Gallagher, Tomasz Sørensen, Jonas Kimura

Published in Northern Journal of Public Health, 2021.

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Petrov, 2014), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a prospective cohort study. Participants were recruited through routine appointments. The primary outcome was depressive symptom score.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Oyelaran, 2016; Szabo et al., 1998).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

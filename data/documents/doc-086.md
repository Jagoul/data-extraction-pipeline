# Pharmacist-led medication review and hypertension: findings from primary care

Freya Kowalski, Ingrid Fischer

Published in Clinical Outcomes Quarterly, 2013.

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Albrecht et al., 2011; Chowdhury et al., 2003), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a prospective cohort study. The analysis included 2,350 participants. The primary outcome was systolic blood pressure at 6 months.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Tanaka et al., 2011; Bianchi et al., 2005).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

# Financial incentives and smoking cessation: findings from three urban hospitals

Nadia Varga, Ama Ivanova, Anna Ibsen, Marta Duarte

Published in Global Health Methods, 2020.
doi:10.5555/njph.2020.9780

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Mensah, 2012), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a cross-sectional survey. A total of 312 participants were included in the analysis. The primary outcome was biochemically verified abstinence.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Lindqvist, 2006; Adeyemi et al., 2006).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

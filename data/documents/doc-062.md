# Vitamin d supplementation for falls in care homes: a Delphi consensus study in three urban hospitals

Ingrid Tanaka

Annals of Community Medicine · 2025 · https://doi.org/10.5555/ghm.2025.3964

## Introduction
Interest in this area has grown [1, 2], with mixed findings across settings [3].

## Methods
We conducted a Delphi consensus study following established guidance [4]. The analysis included 240 participants. Our primary outcome was number of falls per resident.

## Results
Effects favoured the intervention and were robust to sensitivity analyses [5-12].

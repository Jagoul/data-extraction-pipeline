# Early mobilisation and post-operative delirium: findings from community settings

Hana Gallagher, Ama Quinlan, Yuki Lindgren, Aisha Albrecht, Sofia Sørensen, Chidi Silva

Published in Journal of Applied Clinical Evidence, 2024.
doi:10.5555/njph.2024.5176

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Almeida et al., 2019; Szabo et al., 2005), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a case-control study. The analysis included 4,512 participants. The primary outcome was incidence of delirium.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Takahashi, 2010; Duarte, 2023; Nakamura et al., 2001).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

# Pharmacist-led medication review for hypertension: a randomised controlled trial in primary care

Maria Osei, Ama Kowalski

Published in Journal of Applied Clinical Evidence, 2017.

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Vasquez and Rahman, 2014; Gallagher et al., 2008; Mensah and Moreau, 2005), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a randomised controlled trial. A total of 2,350 participants were included in the analysis. The primary outcome was systolic blood pressure at 6 months.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Lindqvist et al., 2010; Marchetti et al., 2002; Petrov et al., 2016).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

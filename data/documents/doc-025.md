# Text-message coaching and type 2 diabetes: findings from primary care

Marta Obi, Kwame Novotny, Clara Silva

Published in Evidence and Practice, 2022.
doi:10.5555/jace.2022.0543

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Kimura and Haddad, 2000; Almeida and Gallagher, 2004; Silva et al., 2002), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a systematic review. We identified 14 eligible studies.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Holloway and Kowalski, 2012; Nakamura et al., 2008; Tanaka et al., 2016).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

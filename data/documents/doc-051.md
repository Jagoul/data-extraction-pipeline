# Pharmacist-led medication review for hypertension: a qualitative interview study in community settings

Ingrid Lindqvist, Diego Tanaka

Journal of Applied Clinical Evidence · 2013

## Introduction
Interest in this area has grown [1, 2], with mixed findings across settings [3].

## Methods
We conducted a qualitative interview study following established guidance [4]. The analysis included 118 participants.

## Results
Effects favoured the intervention and were robust to sensitivity analyses [5-12].

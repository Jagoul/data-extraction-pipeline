# Light-exposure scheduling for sleep problems in shift workers: a prospective cohort study in rural clinics

Hana Marchetti

Published in Evidence and Practice, 2020.
doi:10.5555/ghm.2020.4494

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Varga et al., 2002; Oyelaran and Haddad, 2014; Novotny, 2001), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a prospective cohort study. The analysis included 2,350 participants. The primary outcome was total sleep time.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Novak et al., 1999; Fischer and Eriksen, 2014; Okafor et al., 2005).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

# Early mobilisation and post-operative delirium: findings from primary care

Ama Tanaka, Ravi Chowdhury

Published in Journal of Applied Clinical Evidence, 2025.

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Albrecht et al., 2011), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a cross-sectional survey. The analysis included 1,204 participants. The primary outcome was incidence of delirium.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Novotny et al., 2015; Adeyemi and Novotny, 2009).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

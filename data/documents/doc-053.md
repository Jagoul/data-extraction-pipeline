# School-based inhaler education and childhood asthma: findings from three urban hospitals

Maria Hartmann, Elena Varga, Leila Ibsen, Kenji Haddad, Omar Ivanova, Priya Almeida

Clinical Outcomes Quarterly · 2012 · https://doi.org/10.5555/jace.2012.2084

## Introduction
Interest in this area has grown [1, 2], with mixed findings across settings [3].

## Methods
We conducted a prospective cohort study following established guidance [4]. A total of 486 participants were included in the analysis. Our primary outcome was emergency visits within 1 year.

## Results
Effects favoured the intervention and were robust to sensitivity analyses [5-7].

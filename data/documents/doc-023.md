# Telephone befriending and depression in older adults: findings from primary care

Freya Laurent, Omar Kimura, Zanele Brennan, Aisha Yamada, Kenji Petrov

Annals of Community Medicine · 2014 · https://doi.org/10.5555/ghm.2014.8448

## Introduction

## Methods
We conducted a qualitative interview study following established guidance [4]. The analysis included 2,350 participants.

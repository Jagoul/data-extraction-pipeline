PRESS RELEASE — September 2022

New research: Early mobilisation for post-operative delirium: a randomised controlled trial in community settings

A randomised controlled trial led by Dr Kenji Novotny and colleagues including Dr Elena Takahashi suggests that the programme could benefit patients.
The team followed 486 participants.
The researchers mainly measured incidence of delirium.
The study was funded by the Alder Institute for Public Health.
The full paper is available from the journal.

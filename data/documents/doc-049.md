# Financial incentives for smoking cessation: a prospective cohort study in three urban hospitals

Nadia Hartmann

Journal of Applied Clinical Evidence · 2013

## Introduction
Interest in this area has grown [1, 2], with mixed findings across settings [3].

## Methods
We conducted a prospective cohort study following established guidance [4]. Participants were recruited through routine appointments. Our primary outcome was biochemically verified abstinence.

## Results
Effects favoured the intervention and were robust to sensitivity analyses [5-8].

PRESS RELEASE — May 2022

New research: Light-exposure scheduling for sleep problems in shift workers: a qualitative interview study in primary care

A qualitative interview study led by Dr Freya Hartmann and colleagues including Dr Leila Hartmann suggests that the programme could benefit patients.
The team followed 2,350 participants.
The study was funded by the Northbridge Health Foundation.
The full paper is available from the journal.

# Vitamin d supplementation for falls in care homes: a prospective cohort study in three urban hospitals

Yuki Ferreira, Ravi Kowalski, Rafael Lindgren, Mateo Romero, Zanele Novotny, Ingrid Oyelaran

Published in Clinical Outcomes Quarterly, 2013.

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Lindqvist et al., 1998; Lindgren et al., 2004), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a prospective cohort study. A total of 240 participants were included in the analysis. The primary outcome was number of falls per resident.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Novak et al., 2004; Brennan and Holloway, 2010).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

# Telephone befriending for depression in older adults: a discrete choice experiment in three urban hospitals

Kwame Laurent, Felix Kowalski, Priya Lindgren, Kwame Delacroix, Nadia Kimura, Siobhan Sørensen

Global Health Methods · 2015

## Introduction
Interest in this area has grown [1, 2], with mixed findings across settings [3].

## Methods
We conducted a discrete choice experiment following established guidance [4]. Participants were recruited through routine appointments. Our primary outcome was depressive symptom score.

## Results
Effects favoured the intervention and were robust to sensitivity analyses [5-9].

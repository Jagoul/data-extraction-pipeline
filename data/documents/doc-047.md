# Text-message coaching for type 2 diabetes: a meta-analysis of randomised trials in community settings

Maria Varga, Sofia Silva, Ingrid Obi, Felix Brennan

Journal of Applied Clinical Evidence · 2018

## Introduction
Interest in this area has grown [1, 2], with mixed findings across settings [3].

## Methods
We conducted a meta-analysis of randomised trials following established guidance [4]. A total of 4,512 participants were included in the analysis. Our primary outcome was change in HbA1c at 12 months.

## Results
Effects favoured the intervention and were robust to sensitivity analyses [5-11].

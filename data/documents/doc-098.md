# Remote weight monitoring for heart failure: a health technology assessment in three urban hospitals

Aisha Achterberg, Kwame Castellanos, Ingrid Ivanova

Northern Journal of Public Health · 2022

## Introduction
Interest in this area has grown [1, 2], with mixed findings across settings [3].

## Methods
We conducted a health technology assessment following established guidance [4]. A total of 118 participants were included in the analysis. Our primary outcome was 30-day readmission.

## Results
Effects favoured the intervention and were robust to sensitivity analyses [5-7].

## Evidence summary: Text-message coaching for type 2 diabetes: a randomised controlled trial in three urban hospitals

Authors: Anna Oyelaran; Kwame Castellanos; Nadia Sørensen; Clara Duarte

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Primary outcome | change in HbA1c at 12 months |
| Year | 2020 |
| Design | RCT |
| Funding | the Meridian Charitable Trust |
| Participants | 2,350 |
| DOI | Not reported |

Summary: the intervention was associated with improved outcomes; see sources below.

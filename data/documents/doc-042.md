PRESS RELEASE — June 2012

New research: School-based inhaler education for childhood asthma: a cross-sectional survey in primary care

A cross-sectional survey led by Dr Marta Eriksen and colleagues including Dr Rafael Yamada suggests that the programme could benefit patients.
The team followed 486 participants.
The researchers mainly measured emergency visits within 1 year.
The study was funded by the Alder Institute for Public Health.
The full paper is available from the journal.

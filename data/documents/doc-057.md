## Evidence summary: Family-based lifestyle coaching and adolescent obesity: findings from community settings

Authors: Mateo Castellanos; Ama Oyelaran; Zanele Moreau

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Design | Case-control |
| Participants | 486 |
| Funding | the Riverside University Hospital research fund |
| DOI | 10.5555/jace.2020.6043 |
| Year | 2020 |
| Primary outcome | change in BMI z-score |

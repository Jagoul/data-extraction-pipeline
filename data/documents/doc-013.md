# Financial incentives and smoking cessation: findings from rural clinics

Rafael Mensah, Felix Holloway

Evidence and Practice · 2015 · https://doi.org/10.5555/coq.2015.7959

## Introduction
Interest in this area has grown [1, 2], with mixed findings across settings [3].

## Methods
We conducted a qualitative interview study following established guidance [4]. A total of 118 participants were included in the analysis.

## Results
Effects favoured the intervention and were robust to sensitivity analyses [5-14].

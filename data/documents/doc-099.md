# Family-based lifestyle coaching for adolescent obesity: a systematic review in rural clinics

Ravi Kaur, Siobhan Tanaka, Mateo Obi, Sofia Delacroix, Siobhan Oyelaran, Diego Mensah

Published in Global Health Methods, 2021.
doi:10.5555/ghm.2021.9558

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Romero and Quinlan, 2005), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a systematic review. We identified 17 eligible studies. The primary outcome was change in BMI z-score.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Delacroix et al., 2009; Castellanos et al., 2015).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

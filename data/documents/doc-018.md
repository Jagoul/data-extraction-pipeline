# Group physiotherapy and chronic low back pain: findings from primary care

Felix Nakamura, Sofia Achterberg, Mateo Duarte, Aisha Kowalski

Clinical Outcomes Quarterly · 2023 · https://doi.org/10.5555/ghm.2023.5198

## Introduction
Interest in this area has grown [1, 2], with mixed findings across settings [3].

## Methods
We conducted a randomised controlled trial following established guidance [4]. Participants were recruited through routine appointments. Our primary outcome was disability score at 3 months.

## Results
Effects favoured the intervention and were robust to sensitivity analyses [5-11].

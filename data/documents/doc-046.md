# Telephone befriending for depression in older adults: a randomised controlled trial in rural clinics

Lucas Gallagher, Jonas Mensah, Sofia Mbeki

Published in Annals of Community Medicine, 2016.
doi:10.5555/njph.2016.5138

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Holloway et al., 2010), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a randomised controlled trial. A total of 4,512 participants were included in the analysis. The primary outcome was depressive symptom score.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Osei, 2011; Haddad and Albrecht, 1999).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

# Pharmacist-led medication review and hypertension: findings from community settings

Elena Fischer, Leila Mbeki, Jonas Rahman

Published in Global Health Methods, 2020.

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Bianchi et al., 2006; Whitfield et al., 2007; Duarte and Achterberg, 2016), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a systematic review. We identified 13 eligible studies. The primary outcome was systolic blood pressure at 6 months.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Yamada and Tanaka, 2010; Delacroix et al., 2013; Quinlan et al., 2017).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

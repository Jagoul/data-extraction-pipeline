# Early mobilisation and post-operative delirium: findings from three urban hospitals

Lucas Lindqvist, Siobhan Moreau, Maria Lindqvist

Published in Northern Journal of Public Health, 2014.
doi:10.5555/ghm.2014.5714

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Novotny and Chowdhury, 2002; Quinlan et al., 1999; Okafor et al., 2000), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a cross-sectional survey. The analysis included 2,350 participants. The primary outcome was incidence of delirium.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Duarte et al., 2011; Novak et al., 2010; Gallagher et al., 2007).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

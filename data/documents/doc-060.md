PRESS RELEASE — January 2014

New research: Early mobilisation for post-operative delirium: a prospective cohort study in primary care

A prospective cohort study led by Dr Kwame Mensah and colleagues including Dr Priya Ncube suggests that the programme could benefit patients.
The team followed 486 participants.
The researchers mainly measured incidence of delirium.
The study was funded by Helix Therapeutics Ltd.
The full paper is available from the journal.

PRESS RELEASE — March 2019

New research: Clinician audit and feedback and antibiotic prescribing: findings from community settings

A prospective cohort study led by Dr Ravi Laurent and colleagues including Dr Rafael Ibsen suggests that the programme could benefit patients.
The team followed 118 participants.
The researchers mainly measured antibiotic prescription rate.
The study was funded by the Northbridge Health Foundation.
The full paper is available from the journal.

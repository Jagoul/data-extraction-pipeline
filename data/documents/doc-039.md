# Remote weight monitoring and heart failure: findings from rural clinics

Marta Almeida, Hana Takahashi, Mateo Takahashi, Marta Marchetti, Chidi Whitfield

Annals of Community Medicine · 2018 · https://doi.org/10.5555/coq.2018.8203

## Introduction
Interest in this area has grown [1, 2], with mixed findings across settings [3].

## Methods
We conducted a prospective cohort study following established guidance [4]. The analysis included 118 participants. Our primary outcome was 30-day readmission.

## Results
Effects favoured the intervention and were robust to sensitivity analyses [5-11].

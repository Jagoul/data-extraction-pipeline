# Telephone befriending and depression in older adults: findings from community settings

Ravi Mbeki, Anna Kowalski, Kenji Okafor

Published in Annals of Community Medicine, 2020.
doi:10.5555/coq.2020.2078

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Achterberg, 2007), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a Delphi consensus study. The analysis included 60 participants. The primary outcome was depressive symptom score.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Okafor et al., 1999; Duarte et al., 2001).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

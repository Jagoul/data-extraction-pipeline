# Vitamin d supplementation for falls in care homes: a qualitative interview study in primary care

Mateo Mbeki, Ama Ferreira, Omar Marchetti

Evidence and Practice · 2025 · https://doi.org/10.5555/ghm.2025.6141

## Introduction
Interest in this area has grown [1, 2], with mixed findings across settings [3].

## Methods
We conducted a qualitative interview study following established guidance [4]. The analysis included 312 participants.

## Results
Effects favoured the intervention and were robust to sensitivity analyses [5-14].

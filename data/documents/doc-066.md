# Telephone befriending for depression in older adults: a prospective cohort study in three urban hospitals

Kwame Vasquez, Elena Szabo, Tomasz Almeida, Noor Kaur, Lucas Tanaka

Published in Clinical Outcomes Quarterly, 2015.
doi:10.5555/ghm.2015.3404

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Lindgren et al., 2000; Kowalski, 2003), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a prospective cohort study. The analysis included 486 participants. The primary outcome was depressive symptom score.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Mensah et al., 2011; Whitfield et al., 2007; Ivanova et al., 2014).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

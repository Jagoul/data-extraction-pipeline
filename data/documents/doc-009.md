New research: Clinician audit and feedback for antibiotic prescribing: a prospective cohort study in primary care

A prospective cohort study led by Dr Ingrid Nakamura and colleagues including Dr Freya Novotny suggests that the programme could benefit patients.
The team followed 486 participants.
The researchers mainly measured antibiotic prescription rate.

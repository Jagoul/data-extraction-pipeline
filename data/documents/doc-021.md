# Financial incentives for smoking cessation: a case-control study in three urban hospitals

Marta Chowdhury, Lucas Fischer, Lucas Novotny

Global Health Methods · 2019 · https://doi.org/10.5555/njph.2019.1483

## Introduction
Interest in this area has grown [1, 2], with mixed findings across settings [3].

## Methods
We conducted a case-control study following established guidance [4]. The analysis included 60 participants. Our primary outcome was biochemically verified abstinence.

## Results
Effects favoured the intervention and were robust to sensitivity analyses [5-11].

# Early mobilisation for post-operative delirium: a qualitative interview study in primary care

Jonas Albrecht, Lucas Nakamura


**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Ivanova, 2010), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a qualitative interview study. A total of 24 participants were included in the analysis.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Kimura and Quinlan, 2016; Quinlan and Haddad, 2021).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

# Light-exposure scheduling and sleep problems in shift workers: findings from primary care

Siobhan Oyelaran, Ravi Whitfield, Aisha Ncube, Maria Duarte

Northern Journal of Public Health · 2019

## Introduction
Interest in this area has grown [1, 2], with mixed findings across settings [3].

## Methods
We conducted a randomised controlled trial following established guidance [4]. A total of 312 participants were included in the analysis. Our primary outcome was total sleep time.

## Results
Effects favoured the intervention and were robust to sensitivity analyses [5-13].

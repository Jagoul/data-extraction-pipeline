# Telephone befriending for depression in older adults: a qualitative interview study in rural clinics

Rafael Brennan, Aisha Ibsen, Noor Moreau, Rafael Haddad, Leila Kaur

Published in Journal of Applied Clinical Evidence, 2025.
doi:10.5555/coq.2025.6752

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Achterberg et al., 2011; Ivanova and Ivanova, 2014), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a qualitative interview study. The analysis included 2,350 participants.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Varga et al., 2018; Quinlan and Oyelaran, 2016; Haddad et al., 2006).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

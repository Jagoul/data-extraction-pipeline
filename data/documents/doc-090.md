# Vitamin d supplementation and falls in care homes: findings from three urban hospitals

Ingrid Tanaka, Rafael Albrecht, Kenji Ivanova

Published in Global Health Methods, 2016.
doi:10.5555/jace.2016.6094

**Background.** Previous work has examined this question (Fischer, 1998), but evidence remains inconsistent.

**Methods.** We conducted a randomised controlled trial. The analysis included 240 participants. The primary outcome was number of falls per resident.

**Results.** The intervention group showed a clinically meaningful improvement compared with usual care, consistent with earlier reports (Almeida and Holloway, 1998; Tanaka and Petrov, 2003).

**Conclusions.** The approach is feasible and merits wider evaluation.

# Remote weight monitoring for heart failure: a systematic review in three urban hospitals

Nadia Albrecht, Tomasz Haddad, Hana Takahashi, Siobhan Vasquez

Northern Journal of Public Health · 2015 · https://doi.org/10.5555/jace.2015.1458

## Introduction
Interest in this area has grown [1, 2], with mixed findings across settings [3].

## Methods
We conducted a systematic review following established guidance [4]. We identified 18 eligible studies. Our primary outcome was 30-day readmission.

## Results
Effects favoured the intervention and were robust to sensitivity analyses [5-10].
